## Selected production approach

The installed Tesseract 0.1.0 plugin animates, composites, edits and renders supplied assets. It does not provide text-to-video, image-to-video, or an avatar-generation service.

Recommended route: use ImageGen to create reference-matched adult/interior assets and discrete action poses, then use Tesseract native layers, keyframes, masks, camera moves and frame sequences. Keep Ollie, sheep and landscape assets unchanged. This creates deliberately staged 2D animation. It does not automatically synthesize continuous walking, hand articulation, or page-turn motion from a still image.

Alternative: generate illustrated motion clips in a separate AI video service, then edit them in Tesseract. No such service is currently established for this task. This route requires access to that service and a consistency review of its output.
