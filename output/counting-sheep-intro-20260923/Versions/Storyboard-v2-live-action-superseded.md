## The first test — 7 seconds, not the full film

The test covers the actual visual hinge of the film, with a little extra reading time. Its timing is exploratory; the approved treatment will be conformed into the 13–21 s section above.

- **0–1.5 s:** warm live-action doorway/shelf shot. The phone is placed outside the bedroom, with the actual active Counting Sheep journey visible. Retain the real shelf contact.
- **1.5–2.6 s:** the hand leaves; closer screen insert. The authentic screen recording is legible and supplies the exact framing for the next cut. If a screen replacement is needed, use the native corner-pin/mask tools and check hand occlusion. Prefer clean on-camera footage or a motivated cut to a screen insert.
- **2.6–3.3 s:** match cut from the screen's landscape to the same production artwork. Align horizon, palette, and Ollie position; a short native transform extends the view. No generated replacement illustration.
- **3.3–7 s:** Ollie's production run cycle follows the landscape. **You wind down. Ollie follows the trail.** holds on screen. No progress meter, clock acceleration, sheep discovery, or implied reward for phone placement.

Review the actual rendered test for: human/art continuity; whether the cut feels like entering this app's world; exact asset fidelity; readable screen and central line; restraint of motion; sound-on and muted comprehension. Deliver MP4, editable Tesseract source, and a filmstrip. **Pause for founder feedback on this test before completing the film.**

## Human footage needed

All shots use one adult and one home. Warm practical evening light, natural skin, quiet performance. A locked or gently operated camera is preferable to stylised handheld movement. Leave 2–3 seconds of handles before and after actions. Landscape 4K/30 fps is useful for reframing; consistent frame rate and white balance matter more than camera choice. Record clean room tone and contact sounds.

## Evidence and sound boundaries

The cumulative search contract is `docs/DECISIONS/ADR-0020-cumulative-farm-credit.md`: time carries across eligible sessions; the seven-hour Wind Down trail accepts separately accounted bedtime bonuses; a search may return a sheep or a clue. The film must not present a bonus as factual time, silently accelerate an active meter, or promise a sheep for every session.

No promise of improved sleep; no measurement of sleep, routine completion, continuous offline behaviour, or phone placement. The shelf action is a human choice in a filmed scene. Keep the central line legible without sound, and make the later return explicit in words as well as lighting/clothing.

Sound is built from real scrolling/contact/page/lamp recordings when available. Add restrained native audio layers for the illustrated world only where they serve the transition. No narration is assumed. Do not use a notification-like chime for every UI step. Preserve quiet and review the encoded test's sound, including its head and tail.
