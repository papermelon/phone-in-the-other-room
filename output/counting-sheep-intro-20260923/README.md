# Counting Sheep launch-film style test

The current deliverable is **Style-Transition-Test-Reels.mp4** (9 seconds, 1080×1920,
30 fps, H.264/AAC), with editable **Style-Transition-Test-Reels.tsrct** and
**Previews/reels-style-test-filmstrip.png**. The full 39-second film is paused for founder
feedback. See **Storyboard/Launch-film-storyboard.md** for the revised story and
remaining capture requirements.

The room uses the portrait AI-generated paper/gouache revision. The Shepherd is
exported from the current native production paths/palette/texture, with film-only
arm poses; no retired raster character is used. Ollie and the Prairie landscape
are original production PNGs; Ollie wears the exact native moss bandana shown in the app capture. Text, phone, timing, image layers and audio remain
editable in Tesseract 0.1.0.

The app insert is a full current Wind Down view from the isolated local Screenbook
fixture with external services disabled. It remains inside a visible phone frame
and is labelled In Counting Sheep. The simulated status bar matches the fixed
10:15 PM fixture; the timer and app UI are unmodified. It is not a recording of a genuine
Wind Down start. No reward/discovery is presented in this test. The screen-to-world
transition fades the framed phone as the matching landscape expands behind it.

Review: inspected placement, arm withdrawal, screen-insert crop, scene boundary,
run-cycle frames and final caption in rendered filmstrips. The landscape predecessor was played through in QuickTime; the portrait export
was verified by filmstrip and encoded-frame extraction. Full portrait playback
review remains pending: QuickTime file-dialog automation did not open the new file,
and Chrome was unavailable. No complete at-speed portrait playback review is claimed. Stream inspection confirms duration, resolution,
frame rate and audio. One synthetic shelf-contact accent at 0.7 seconds measures
−24.45 dBTP; the remaining quiet is intentional. No human listening assessment or
physical-phone sound check is claimed.

Authoring scripts, final document snapshots, provenance and the loudness report
are in `.tesseract-work/`. `reels-current.json` records
the final portrait composition, including the corrected caption positions and evening capture.
