# Corgi Ollie breed study — 23 September 2026

These are concept masters, **not** a selectable breed. The first attempted sprite kept
Border Collie anatomy and markings while changing fur colour and ear size. It is rejected.

The intended breed is a Pembroke Welsh Corgi. Keep Ollie's purple eyes and the app's
simple, paper-textured gouache layers, but give this variant a distinct identity:

- Long, low barrel torso and short sturdy legs in sitting, running, lying and rising poses.
- Broad, fox-like wedge head with proportionate upright ears. Large ears are fine for a
  corgi; enlarging ears on a Border Collie head is not a breed change.
- Warm copper/ginger fur, a darker reddish saddle, ivory lower cheeks and muzzle,
  chest, underside and paw tips. The forehead stays uninterrupted copper: no dot,
  bindi, fleck, or broad white Border Collie blaze down the face.
- Short rounded fluffy bobtail in every pose, never Ollie's long Collie plume.
- Same character at every scale and state, not separate inconsistent illustrations.

The [sit](corgi-sit.png), [run](corgi-run.png), and [rest](corgi-rest.png) masters
explore that anatomy. Before adding a picker or public Campfire breed ID, complete and
inspect all 23 existing Ollie pose equivalents (Home blink/tilt, Farm idle, run, settle,
rest, rise, gesture), registered neckline/ground points and every fitted garment.
Check silhouette without colour and at actual Home/Farm/Campfire sizes. Reject frames
whose proportions or markings drift back toward the base Border Collie.
