Approved poses, not finished animations. Earlier sideways, floppy and nearly earless variants remain rejected. The playable sheet demonstrates BODY TIMING ONLY and still has unapproved ears; it must be rebuilt from the approved photo-based reference. The ear and tongue candidates are separate stills. Production frames need consistent proportions, registration, true alpha, transitions and matching cosmetic layers for Home and Farm.

Layout and motion decisions
  • One hero, two ornaments, no illustrated room or scenery panel.
  • Phone-away and phone-wake times stay visible; bed and wake sit inside the plan disclosure.
  • People/statuses plus one highlight; full history remains in Slumber Party.
  • Personal Ollie is the chosen direction. The shared scene is deferred; a shared Farm can be explored later.
  • Your own Shepherd is an optional composition study, not a selected default. Keep Ollie prominent.
  • Production motion should choose occasional actions with long rests, pause offscreen, and honour Reduce Motion.

Design comparison only. Native implementation and verification are tracked separately.
