# Slumber Party copy inventory

This inventory covers the release-facing Slumber Party strings revised for the shared
commitment. Copy is warm, concise, literal, and avoids medical promises, shame, and internal
implementation language.

| State / location | Old copy | Proposed copy | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home/Farm card, invitation | “Wind down with a small flock” / “Seven quiet nights with people you invite. Only tucked-away phones and completed mornings are shared.” | “Slumber Party” / “Choose a Wind Down goal together” / “Invite people you know, share what helps, and keep one another going for seven nights.” | States the purpose before the farm metaphor and matches the product contract. |
| Landing | “A little flock, a little quiet” | “2–8 PEOPLE · 7 NIGHTS” / “Wind down together” / “Choose one bedtime goal as a group. Keep your own routine, share the parts that help, and encourage one another along the way.” | Makes group size, duration, and individual autonomy scannable. |
| First entry, step 1 | No clear shared-goal explanation | “Choose one goal together” / “Your group agrees on one thing to practise for seven nights.” | Explains the commitment in one tired-reader-sized step. |
| First entry, step 2 | “Share the quiet” | “Keep your own routine” / “Bedtimes and Wind Down routines can be different. Share only the ideas you want others to see.” | Separates group goal from private schedule and routine. |
| First entry, step 3 | “Cheer each other on” | “Help one another follow through” / “See nightly progress and send a small cheer. There are no rankings.” | Sets supportive expectations without competition. |
| First entry, step 4 | “Keep it private” | “You control what is shared” / “By joining, the group can see your name, whether you followed through, and rounded minutes. Sleep duration and how rested you felt stay off unless you turn them on.” | Makes default-on fields and sleep opt-ins explicit before the party starts. |
| Join / create lobby | Goal title only | “By joining, this group can see your name, whether you followed through, rounded Wind Down and Phone Away minutes, and whether your phone was tucked away. Sleep duration and how rested you felt stay off unless you turn them on.” | Clear join consent for default-on minutes before redemption. |
| Shared goal tip | No goal-specific context | “Everyone chose this same goal. Your bedtime and routine can still be your own.” | Prevents the shared goal from sounding like a shared schedule. |
| My setup | “Set up your flock” | “Set up the shared goal on this iPhone. App choices stay here.” | Clarifies local setup and data boundary. |
| Instagram setup | “Shield Instagram” | “Choose apps on this iPhone” / “You confirm Instagram is included. Counting Sheep can only report coarse app-shielding observation.” | Does not claim the server can inspect an opaque Family Controls token. |
| Group progress | “Shared moments” / “Ollie is keeping watch” | “Our seven nights” / “See how the group is following through and send a small cheer.” | Removes redundant empty-state cards and presents named team progress. |
| Private status | “No check-in” | “No update shared” / “No update shared is not counted as completion.” | Honest status that is not mistaken for success. |
| Routine ideas | “Share a tip” | “What helps our group wind down” / “Share the ideas that help you. Different routines are expected.” | Limits sharing to bundled guidance ideas, not free-form posting. |
| Evidence | “Learn more” | “Ideas and sources” / “These are general sleep-health ideas from Counting Sheep’s source library.” | Identifies the source library without clinical promises. |
| Invite | “Invite flockmates” | “Invite people you know. The seven nights begin after everyone has joined, accepted the goal, and the host starts.” | Explains the lobby gate and avoids unexplained pasture language. |
| Code actions | “Send invite” | “Copy code” / “Share code” / “Create a reusable code” | Provides explicit, accessible actions for workshops. |
| Sharing/account | “Privacy” | “Sharing and account” / “Share my Wind Down minutes” / “Share my Phone Away minutes” / “Share sleep duration” / “Share how rested I felt” | Names the independent controls without HealthKit or token language. |
| Shielding | “Shielding observed” | “App shielding was observed” | Matches the coarse observation claim. |
| Completion card | “adds no wool, sheep, rank, or reward” | “Shared nights can bring a little wool, a small keepsake, or one Slumber Party sheep. Reactions do not.” | Replaces the outdated no-reward claim. |
| Sources tip | No source context | “These are general sleep-health ideas from Counting Sheep’s source library.” | Honest, concise, and non-medical. |

The old terms remain only in compatibility identifiers, historical docs, or secondary legacy
views where needed for decoding. They are not used to explain the current shared commitment.
