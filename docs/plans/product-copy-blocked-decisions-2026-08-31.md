# Product-copy blocked decisions — 2026-08-31

This register contains only decisions that cannot be resolved by copy editing or local source
validation. Current release presentation must remain factual and hide capability-gated UI until
the corresponding decision, agreement, implementation, and release validation are complete.

## Founder and operations decisions

### Slumber Party reports and moderation outcomes

- **Affected surfaces:** accepted-report confirmation in Slumber Party detail, blocking and
  reporting help, support/error recovery, and the public-policy source.
- **Current safe treatment:** confirm that a report was accepted for review, explain what local
  blocking changes, and promise neither a response time nor a moderation result.
- **Viable decisions:** keep an acknowledgement-only reporting channel; establish a documented
  triage process with bounded response language; or add a staffed safety-escalation path with its
  own operating commitments.

### Party-history retention, withdrawal, and deletion

- **Affected surfaces:** join disclosure, ordinary leave, leader dissolve, future archive UI,
  account deletion, reopening an Apple-owned account, and the public-policy source.
- **Current safe treatment:** describe current sharing and access without presenting the planned
  party-lifetime archive or deletion behavior as deployed.
- **Viable decisions:** retain attributed shared history for the party lifetime subject to a
  reviewed deletion process; replace eligible rows with privacy-preserving tombstones; or define
  narrower retention windows and redaction rules. Each option needs privacy, legal, and operations
  review before product copy can promise it.

### Group-leader powers and friendly competition

- **Affected surfaces:** create/join disclosure, member roles, round summary, accountability
  prompts, and any future family or leader controls.
- **Current safe treatment:** present invite-only adult groups and factual seven-night round
  activity only. Do not imply that a leader can alter another adult's plan, permissions, or app
  limits, and do not expose scoring that has not been specified.
- **Viable decisions:** organizer-only administration; bounded round coordination without
  enforcement; or a separately agreed competition model with explicit scoring and tie rules.
  Child accounts and negotiated parent/child enforcement require a separate product decision.
