# Home, routines, and receipts — 22 September 2026

## Current direction and implementation

Preserve Ollie’s animated personal home. The founder’s Atoms, Alan, and Calm Sleep references inform one connected hero/intent area followed by Your evening, Your day, and Offline Together. Atoms contributes an intentional, prominent start action and press feedback; Alan contributes character continuity; Calm contributes section hierarchy and legible plan values. The hero shows the scheduled before-bed minutes, explicitly labelled Your planned Wind Down. Your evening retains the separate Before bed/After waking plan values. There is no invented sleep-readiness score, new habit-completion claim, forced long press, or reward rule.

Home names Start Wind Down during its window or when the default 30-minute Phone Away would overlap its start; otherwise Start Phone Away. The icon sits beside the existing text to reduce button height, with natural wrapping at large text sizes. This manual lead-in is rechecked at confirmation and never advances automatic starts. It presents the whole saved evening routine or the Phone Away purpose. Its compact Phone Away card opens the planner. That route is intentional secondary planning, not the only way to start Phone Away. Both social entries have independent info controls explaining private Slumber Party groups and Campfire’s Global community, without changing visibility. Farm retains the separate seven-hour Wind Down and 100-minute Phone Away search meters; Home’s duplicate meter is removed.

The goal sheet now presents one identifiable route carrying its goal/plan and edit token atomically. It no longer presents a Boolean-controlled sheet whose content can be absent on the first frame. Existing account/edit-token fences remain.

Routine planning includes phase-specific ideas, rationale, examples, and source notes. The idea browser owns a temporary draft, uses the existing bounded add/replace rules, and commits only on Save ideas. Onboarding still saves into its enclosing onboarding draft. Back discards an unsaved ideas draft. Related-source navigation retains the same draft and phase.

The founder selected **Farm progress** as the release-facing term. Persisted credit names, minutes, wool, search thresholds, and accounting remain unchanged. Receipts show timer duration and Farm progress first, with Timer & progress details and Health & app protection collapsed by default.
