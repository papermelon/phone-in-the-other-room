# Wind Down Guidance Sources

This is the source register for the locally bundled Counting Sheep guidance library. The app uses
short, cautious paraphrases and keeps the full register reachable from the secondary
**“About these ideas and sources”** link. This is general sleep-health education, not insomnia
treatment. It does not reproduce clinical questionnaires or present the content as medical care.

## Sources

- `nhlbi-healthy-sleep` — National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, “Healthy Sleep
  Habits”: <https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep-deprivation/healthy-sleep-habits>
- `nhlbi-sleep-wake-cycle` — National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, “How Sleep Works:
  Your Sleep/Wake Cycle”: <https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/sleep/sleep-wake-cycle>
- `nhlbi-circadian-treatment` — National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute, “Circadian
  Rhythm Disorders: Treatment”: <https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/circadian-rhythm-disorders/treatment>
- `aasm-cbt-i` — Edinger et al., American Academy of Sleep Medicine clinical practice
  guideline for behavioral and psychological treatments of chronic insomnia (reference context,
  not a treatment protocol in Counting Sheep):
  <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7853203/>
- `va-stimulus-control` — Veterans Health Library, “Understanding CBT-I: Using Your Bed
  Only for Sleep” (reference context, not a treatment protocol in Counting Sheep):
  <https://veteranshealthlibrary.va.gov/Encyclopedia/142%2C41435_VA>
- `counting-sheep-principles` — `docs/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md` and `AGENTS.md`, for the
  phone-away ritual and non-coercive product boundaries.
- `counting-sheep-booklet` — the local early Counting Sheep wellness booklet. It is used
  only as an internal design reference for routine mapping and reflective prompts; its
  clinical assessment, diary, checklist, and outcome claims are not shipped.

## Editorial boundaries

- General sleep-health guidance is distinct from insomnia treatment. The app does not diagnose,
  treat, or claim to review chronic insomnia, and this register does not claim that qualified
  clinical review has occurred. Any future treatment-adjacent or personalized experiment needs
  qualified sleep/CBT-I review before external release.

- Say “may help,” “can make room for,” or “try noticing,” never “will improve sleep.”
- Do not diagnose insomnia or infer a disorder from HealthKit, Screen Time, or reflections.
- Do not prescribe sleep restriction, sleep-efficiency targets, medication, supplements,
  or a fixed rule for leaving bed.
- Educational notifications are allowed only when separately enabled by the person: use
  one source-linked, locally bundled, deterministic tip per night, replacing an existing
  midpoint or bedtime cue. Never send guidance overnight; an overnight notification may
  only be a generic, usage-triggered reminder after selected-app activity. Require
  qualified sleep/CBT-I review before external release.
- Keep each card short enough for a tired reader and show its source label in the guide.
- The current bundled library includes cautious NHLBI-sourced cards about leaving personally
  suitable room after a heavy meal and trying an earlier personal caffeine cutoff. These are
  observations, not universal cutoffs. A future seven-night experiment (morning light, late
  meals, or caffeine timing) remains gated pending qualified sleep/CBT-I review; it must stay
  optional, local-only, non-rewarded, and free of a nightly compliance checklist.
